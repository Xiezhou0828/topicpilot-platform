## Executive result

The audit covered **107 enabled hierarchy topics**, of which **104 topics have any stock coverage** and **97 appear in the PRIMARY pool**. The supplied stock overview contains 507 coded stocks and 719 PRIMARY topic relations; 714 PRIMARY rows match the supplied hierarchy and 5 PRIMARY rows use unmapped labels. This tranche independently reviewed **258 candidates across 97 topic labels** (**93 hierarchy-matched topics and 4 unmapped labels**) using company-owned pages, annual reports/regulatory disclosures and industry publications. It produced **194 CANDIDATE rows** and **64 NEEDS_REVIEW rows**. By topic-set disposition, **49 sets have no NEEDS_REVIEW row** and **48 topics still contain at least one NEEDS_REVIEW decision**. All hierarchy-matched PRIMARY topics now have at least one reviewed candidate; **4 unmapped PRIMARY labels** remain classification-review items, and **461 PRIMARY member rows** remain unreviewed and are not promoted.

No row is APPROVED or production-ready. Importance is assigned only where the evidence supports a durable structural representative; the current-day price fields were not used.

## Methodology

1. Candidate scope is the supplied stock overview, with `主要題材` treated as a provisional PRIMARY/CORE basis; this equivalence still requires PM confirmation.
2. Topic names are kept from the supplied hierarchy; no membership is changed. The supplied hierarchy has no topic ID or slug columns, so `topic_id` is left blank and `topic_slug` preserves the exact supplied `細題材` label; no parallel identifier is invented.
3. Evidence hierarchy: official product/business pages and investor materials; annual reports and public disclosures; reputable industry sources.
4. Each researched candidate was assessed on structural relevance, market recognition, topic sensitivity/participation (not used as identity proof), scale/influence and durability.
5. Only 1.00, 0.75 and 0.50 are used. No candidate was chosen from one-day return, volume or limit-up status.
6. Where a source conflict or topic-adjacency exists, the row remains NEEDS_REVIEW.
